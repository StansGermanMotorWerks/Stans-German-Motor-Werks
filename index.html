Good to know

Questions, answered

Will an independent shop void my factory warranty?

No. Under the federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, a manufacturer can't void your warranty just because routine service or repairs were done outside the dealership. Keep your records — everything here is documented in writing — and your coverage stays intact.

Do you use genuine parts?

Genuine OEM or original-equipment-grade parts, matched to your VIN — the same quality your car left the factory with, without the dealer markup.

How do estimates work?

The car is inspected and tested first. You get the findings and a written estimate, nothing gets replaced until you approve it, and you'll hear honestly what can safely wait.

How do I book?

Routine maintenance — oil, tires, plugs, scheduled service — grab a time on the calendar. Something’s wrong or you’re not sure? Send the form or call / text (323) 810-1649 and Stan will get back with next steps.

What cars do you work on?

German makes exclusively: Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen, and MINI. That focus is the whole point — the right tools, the right parts, and a mechanic who has seen your exact problem before.
