What we do

Everything your car needs, under one roof

From routine oil services to deep engine work, the job is done to factory specification — and explained in plain English before a wrench is turned.

Scheduled maintenance

Oil services, inspections, filters, spark plugs, and fluid exchanges on the manufacturer's schedule — keeping warranties intact and resale value strong.

Diagnostics & check-engine

Factory-grade scan equipment traces faults to the root cause instead of swapping parts on a guess. You get the findings in writing.

Brakes & suspension

Pads, rotors, and wear sensors; struts, control arms, and bushings — restoring the tight, planted feel these cars left the factory with.

Engine & drivetrain

Timing components, gaskets and seals, mounts, and transmission services — the bigger jobs, handled methodically and documented.

Electrical & battery

Battery replacement with proper registration and coding, charging-system repair, and patient chasing of the wiring gremlins German cars are famous for.

Cooling & climate

Water pumps, thermostats, radiators, and expansion tanks, plus full air-conditioning service — before a small leak becomes a warped head.

Pre-purchase inspections

Buying a used German car? A thorough inspection and honest verdict first can save you thousands after. Bring it by before you sign anything.
